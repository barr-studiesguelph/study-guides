## How to use these guides

1. **Read the summary first.** Each guide opens with what you need to know.
2. **Work the examples yourself** before looking at the steps.
3. **Try the practice questions.** Click a question to reveal the answer only after you've tried it.
4. **Use search** (top of the page) to jump straight to a term or formula.

!!! warning "Check against your course"
    These guides support your studying but don't replace your lectures, readings or textbook. If something here conflicts with what your professor taught, go with your professor, and [let me know](about.md#spot-a-mistake) so I can fix it.
